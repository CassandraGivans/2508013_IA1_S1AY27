LLMs: A Tool, Not a Crutch

Large Language Models such as ChatGPT, Claude and Copilot can support students when they are used responsibly. However, students should still understand and complete their own academic work.

LLMs can be useful for explaining difficult concepts, helping students brainstorm ideas, checking understanding and providing examples. They should support learning rather than replace the student's own thinking.

In my view, technology is most useful when it helps a student become more capable instead of making the student dependent on it.
